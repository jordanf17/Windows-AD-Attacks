I have been diving deep into the infrastructure that keeps the world’s most powerful organizations running: Active Directory (AD). Attached are some of my notes covering its import concepts and the common attacks used against it.

🏗️Importance: Active Directory serves as the primary Identity Provider (IdP) for an organization’s internal security. It is the system used to manage and verify every employee, computer, and service within the corporate network.

💸 Market Share: While some believe AD holds only a small piece of the market, that statistic is deceptive. In reality, over 90% of Fortune 500 companies rely on it daily for their operations.

💥 Impact:
A successful attack on AD can lead to severe consequences, including unauthorized access to sensitive information, data breaches, and operational disruptions.

⚔️ Active Directory Attacks: My notes focus on the most common attack vectors, such as DCSync, Golden Tickets, and Kerberos Delegation. I’ve aimed to answer the critical "Real World" questions:
